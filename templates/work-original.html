Case study

KARBONKREDS — turning a complex market into a clear digital experience.

The challenge

Carbon markets are dense, technical and easy to distrust. KARBONKREDS needed a site that felt transparent and Canada-focused, without drowning visitors in jargon — while serving four distinct audiences with different goals.

The approach

We structured the homepage around four clear entry points — buy, sell, develop, invest — paired with a context-first narrative ("confidence begins with context") and a geospatial map showing real Canadian market activity.

What we built

A dark, editorial site with an interactive rotating globe, a live market-intelligence section, and pathway cards that route each visitor to the right conversation — built for clarity over decoration.

Result

A credible, structured market entry point live at karbonkreds.com.
